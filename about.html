About Postmates Research Notes

An independent research publication on Postmates deals for existing users.

What this publication is

This site publishes research, not coupon lists. The main report explains which deal types actually work for existing Postmates users in October 2026, with every claim dated and labeled by evidence type. The Uber One value page works the membership math with real numbers. The referral terms page states the program terms exactly as the app showed them. The sources and method page shows how every claim was produced, so readers can check the work.

The underlying tracking, the live code list with per code check dates, additions, and delistings, is maintained by the source publication. If a claim here and the live list ever differ, the live list is the newer record and this report should be read as the October 2026 snapshot it is dated as.

What this publication is not

  • It is not affiliated with Postmates or Uber, and it is not an official Postmates page.
  • It is not a dumped coupon list. Lists of twenty or thirty codes are misleading for existing users, because most public codes are targeted and fail on accounts they were never issued to.
  • It is not a checkout tester. A community-reported pattern means users reported it, not that it was tested on your account. Only the app can confirm what your account will honor.

How corrections work

Offer terms change without announcement, and a dated snapshot is only useful if its readers can flag where reality has moved. If the app shows you a term this site states differently, note the date you checked and what the app showed, and open an issue on the project repository at github.com/papajohnss482-crypto/postmates-existing-users-guide. Corrections update the record with dates, they are never made silently.
