Is Uber One Worth It on Postmates? The Break-Even Math

Checked in-app on October 2, 2026 and re-checked on October 12, 2026. The trial offer was live and unchanged on both dates.

The offer as shown in the app

  • Free for 4 weeks, then $9.99 per month
  • $0 delivery fee on eligible orders
  • Up to 10 percent off eligible orders
  • 5 percent back on rides
  • The app states members save $29 on average every month. That is Uber's own stated average, not a figure from our research, and heavy users save more than light users by definition

The only question that matters: how often do you order?

Membership pays for itself on fees alone once the delivery fees you would have paid pass $9.99 in a month. Everything after that, the percentage discounts, is upside.

Worked examples

These examples use sample fees to show the math. Your real fees vary by order and city, so run the same arithmetic with the fees on your last few receipts.

1 order per month, $3.99 delivery fee$3.99Not worth it on delivery fees alone. Use the free trial only if you cancel in time.

Mostly grocery orders, 2 per monthVaries by storeBorderline. Check whether your stores carry the $0 delivery fee benefit before deciding.

How this interacts with Meal Deals

The $12 Meal Deals already carry $0 delivery and $0 service fees at participating restaurants, checked in-app in October 2026. If most of your orders are Meal Deals, part of the membership's fee benefit overlaps with value you already get free. In that case the membership decision rests on your non-Meal-Deals orders and the percentage discounts, not on fees.

Our bottom line for existing users

Order three or more times a month at normal fees and Uber One is the most reliable saving available to an existing Postmates user, more reliable than any public code. Order once a month or less and it is not, whatever the trial screen says. Take the 4 week free trial either way if you have not used it, and set a reminder before it converts to the $9.99 monthly price.
